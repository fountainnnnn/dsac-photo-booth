# Brief: Open House photo booth film

- **Product:** the Open House photo booth (SP DSAC; `open-house` branch). Runs on the booth laptop.
- **Audience:** Open House visitors. The film loops on a screen beside the booth.
- **Message:** step in, go anywhere, take the photo home on your phone.
- **Show:** pick a background · type your own (AI-drawn) · avatars on every face · countdown + shutter ·
  scan the QR · save on the phone · deleted from the booth after ten minutes.
- **End card:** "Come strike a pose." over a "Find the photo booth →" pill, then the SP DSAC logo. No URL.
- **Guests:** two illustrated people (Maya, Jun), cut out, no real faces.
- **Length:** ~40s. Font Roboto (the frame's face). Tokens from the app: ink #17161a, stage #0b0a0c, SP red #e1262f
  only where the app uses it (shutter, chosen tile).
- **Real copy used:** "Say cheese." · "Where do you want to be?" · "Make it" · "Making your background…" ·
  "Your photo is ready" · "Scan to download." · "Save your DSAC photo." · "Save photo".
